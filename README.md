## About

I am an **Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (E&TC) student** with a growing interest in electronics, embedded systems, and practical hardware projects.

I enjoy learning how electronic systems work—from sensors and microcontrollers to circuit design and PCB-based solutions. I am particularly interested in building hands-on projects using **Arduino**, sensors, and other electronic components, while steadily expanding my understanding of embedded programming, communication systems, and hardware–software integration.

- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Electronics-6D28D9?style=flat-square&logo=electronegativity&logoColor=white" alt="Electronics" /> **Electronics:** circuit fundamentals, electronic components, measurement tools, and practical experimentation.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Embedded_Systems-4F46E5?style=flat-square&logo=arduino&logoColor=white" alt="Embedded Systems" /> **Embedded Systems:** Arduino, microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and real-world automation projects.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PCB_Design-7C3AED?style=flat-square&logo=pcbway&logoColor=white" alt="PCB Design" /> **PCB & Hardware Design:** exploring schematic design, PCB layouts, component selection, and prototyping.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/IoT-5B21B6?style=flat-square&logo=internetofthings&logoColor=white" alt="IoT" /> **Learning Focus:** IoT, communication technologies, embedded C/C++, and intelligent sensor-based systems.
- <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Goal-4338CA?style=flat-square&logo=target&logoColor=white" alt="Goal" /> **Goal:** grow into an engineer who can design reliable, useful products by connecting electronics, software, and innovation.
