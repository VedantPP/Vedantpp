## About

I am **Vedant Banugade**, an **Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (E&TC) student** with a growing interest in electronics, embedded systems, and practical hardware projects.

I enjoy learning how electronic systems work—from sensors and microcontrollers to circuit design and PCB-based solutions. I am particularly interested in building hands-on projects using **Arduino**, sensors, and other electronic components, while steadily expanding my understanding of embedded programming, communication systems, and hardware–software integration.

- **Electronics:** circuit fundamentals, electronic components, measurement tools, and practical experimentation.
- **Embedded Systems:** Arduino, microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and real-world automation projects.
- **PCB & Hardware Design:** exploring schematic design, PCB layouts, component selection, and prototyping.
- **Learning Focus:** IoT, communication technologies, embedded C/C++, and intelligent sensor-based systems.
- **Goal:** grow into an engineer who can design reliable, useful products by connecting electronics, software, and innovation.

<p align="center">
  <b>Open to:</b> learning opportunities, student collaborations, embedded systems projects, IoT projects, electronics communities, and open-source contributions.
